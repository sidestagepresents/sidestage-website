# About Us

Sidestage Presents is Canada's newest premier music promotion company, bringing the best in hip-hop and EDM events to cities across the country.

We work directly with artists and their teams to put on shows in Toronto, Montreal, Vancouver, Ottawa, and cities across Canada — from intimate club shows to festival stages.

For bookings, partnerships, and show announcements, follow us on Instagram at [@SidestagePresents](https://www.instagram.com/sidestagepresents/).
